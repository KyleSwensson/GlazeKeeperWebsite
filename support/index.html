Contact support

Questions, bug reports, and feature requests are all welcome! You can email me directly, no support form is needed.

Reporting a problem

To help investigate, please include the following when reporting an issue with the app:

  • Your Glaze Keeper version, shown in Settings → About.
  • Your device model and iOS version.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead, including any error message if present.
  • The steps that led to the problem and whether it happens every time.

Screenshots are optional, please remove any private information before sending them. Please do not send your notebook backup or personal photos unless they are absolutely necessary to investigate your request.

Back up before deleting or restoring.

Please be aware that deleting the app will remove all local data. Do not delete and reinstall the app as a first troubleshooting step, as if you have not created a backup it may lead to a loss of all of your pieces and profiles. Please save an exported backup outside the app before either deleting or restoring the app if you want to keep your records and photos.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my notebook stored?

Your records and selected photos are stored locally on your device. Glaze Keeper does not require an account or provide automatic cloud synchronization. Your device may include app data in its own backups, depending on your settings. You can also create an explicit notebook backup using the steps below.

How do I back up my notebook?

Go to Settings → Backup and restore → Create backup. Use the sharing options to save the file outside the app, such as in Files or a cloud storage location you control. The backup includes notebook records and photos. Keep the file somewhere you can find it again.

Exported backups are not encrypted by Glaze Keeper. Store them securely and only share them with recipients you trust.

How do I restore a backup?

First, export your current notebook if you want to keep a copy. Then, go to Settings → Backup and restore → Choose backup to restore, select a Glaze Keeper backup file, and confirm Replace notebook. Restoring replaces the current notebook rather than merging the two.

How do I move my notebook to another device?

Create a backup on the original device, then transfer the file to your new device using a storage or sharing service you choose. Install Glaze Keeper on the new device, then restore from the file using the steps above. Check that your records and photos are present before removing the app or its data from the original device.

How do I delete my notebook?

Go to Settings → Backup and restore → Delete entire notebook. Follow the confirmation instructions and select Permanently delete notebook. This removes the notebook’s records and saved photos. Export a backup first if you want the ability to restore them later.

This does not delete your original photo-library images, exported backup files, or device backups, as these are all managed outside of the scope of the app. Manage those copies separately. You can also use individual records’ deletion controls when you only want to remove particular entries.

Can you recover a lost notebook for me?

As your notebook is only stored locally, if it is lost without a backup there is no way to recover it. If you have an exported backup, you can restore it in the app. A device backup may offer another recovery option through your device’s backup service, depending on what it contains.
