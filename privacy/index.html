Privacy policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains how Glaze Keeper handles information when you use the mobile app or visit this website. In this policy, “we” and “our” refer to Kyle Swensson, the developer of Glaze Keeper.

Your notebook stays under your control.

Glaze Keeper stores your notebook on your device. We do not receive your notebook through the app, require an account, or use advertising or tracking tools. You decide whether to export or share your data.

Information stored in the app

The app saves information you enter or select, including glaze combinations, pottery pieces, clay bodies, firing details, notes, tags, photos, and app preferences. This information is stored locally to organize and display your notebook, remember your preferences, and let you import or export backups.

The app does not send this information to a developer-operated server. It does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics or crash-reporting services, and it does not sell your information.

Photos and permissions

When you add photos, the app uses your device’s photo picker and copies the images you select into its local storage so they can be displayed with your pottery records. Adding photos is optional. The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

You can cancel photo selection and manage any available photo permissions in your device’s Settings. Revoking a permission does not remove copies already saved in your notebook. To remove those copies, delete the associated records or the entire notebook. Photos may retain embedded metadata; the app does not guarantee that metadata is removed.

Backups and sharing

Exporting a backup creates a file containing notebook records and photos and opens your device’s sharing options. You choose where to save or send it. Importing a backup reads a file you select using the system file picker. Temporary import and export files are removed after the operation finishes.

Backup files are not encrypted by Glaze Keeper. Only share them with recipients and services you trust. A service you choose, such as a cloud storage or email provider, handles the copy you send under its own privacy policy. The app does not automatically share your notebook with those services or with us.

Your operating system may include app data in device backups, including iCloud backups, depending on your device settings. This is separate from app-provided cloud synchronization, which Glaze Keeper does not offer. You can manage device backups with your operating system or backup provider.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Your notebook remains in the app’s local storage until you delete it or remove the app and its data. You can delete individual records using the app’s deletion controls, or go to Settings → Backup and restore → Delete entire notebook to remove the notebook’s records and saved photos. App appearance preferences may remain after clearing the notebook.

Deleting the app (rather than offloading it) removes its local data from the device. Neither deleting records nor deleting the app removes original photos from your photo library, previously exported files, copies held by recipients, or device backups. Manage or delete those copies separately with the relevant device, recipient, or service.

We cannot access, recover, or delete your locally stored notebook on your behalf. You can export a copy through the app’s backup feature. There is no Glaze Keeper account to close and no optional analytics or advertising collection to opt out of.

Contacting us

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, and attachments. We use it to respond to your request and resolve support issues, not for advertising. Please do not send your notebook or photos unless they are needed for your request.

We retain correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations. You can use the contact details below to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent us. Our email provider, Google (Gmail), processes correspondence to deliver and store messages. See Google’s Privacy Policy. Any providers handling information on our behalf must protect it consistently with this policy and applicable requirements.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, cookies, contact forms, or tracking scripts to the site. GitHub may process technical information, including your IP address and request details, to provide and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for its practices and privacy choices. Visiting this website does not give GitHub access to your app notebook.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if our practices change and revise the date above. Any new data collection that requires permission will be accompanied by the appropriate notice and consent request.
